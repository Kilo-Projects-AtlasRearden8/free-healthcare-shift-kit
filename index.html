Free Healthcare Shift Kit

Printable worksheets for nurses and healthcare workers · Education only — not medical advice

Education only. These sheets are organizational and learning aids for healthcare workers. They are not medical advice, do not replace clinical judgment or facility policy, and do not create a legal patient record. Always follow your employer's protocols and your state's scope-of-practice rules.

What's in the kit

Four fillable, printable worksheets. Each is available as a standalone HTML file (open in any browser, fill in, print with Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and as Markdown for editing or copying into your own notes.

Brain Sheet

Shift brain: patient snapshot, SBAR-style assessment, vital signs, lines/tubes/drains, IV access, medications, PRN meds, pending labs, and a pre-handoff to-do list.

Open brain-sheet.html
`brain-sheet.md` also included

Shift Handover Template

SBAR-style handoff between offgoing and oncoming nurses: situation, background, assessment, recommendation, pending tasks, follow-ups, and signature block.

CE (Continuing Education) Tracker

Track CE courses, contact hours, cost, completion date, and your license renewal deadline in one printable sheet.

Medication Log

Log medication administrations by shift: time, medication, dose, route, given by, and initials, with a per-shift signature block.

Open medication-log.html
`medication-log.md` also included

How to use

  1. Open any `.html` file directly in your browser. No account, no sign-up, no internet needed once you have the files.
  2. Fill in the fields in the browser.
  3. Print with Ctrl+P / Cmd+P. Print styles hide on-screen controls and buttons for a clean worksheet.
  4. Use the `.md` versions if you prefer a markdown viewer or want to copy sections into your own notes.
